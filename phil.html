(1) Phil Dunphy

Loves magic, trampolines, and connecting with people. He is the 2nd best real estate agent in the LA area. Also, he is truly a talented magician and does great performances at SCARB. He is super goofy, but an extremely supportive dad to all his kids. He's a great father and I feel like he has a lot of emotional intelligence. Sometimes he doesn't get it right at the start, but he does right at the end and really helps his kids grow.
