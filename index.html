(1) Phil Dunphy

Loves magic, trampolines, and connecting with people. He is the 2nd best real estate agent in the LA area. He is super goofy, but an extremely supportive dad to all his kids.

More Info on Phil

(2) Claire Dunphy

Initially used to be a homemaker, but started working for her dad's (Jay) company, Pritchett's Closets & Blinds, once her kids got older. Eventually, she lead the company and merged it with Ezra Vision. I respect that despite having an extremely overbearing mom (Dede), she never treated her own kids as badly as she was treated. I will admit she is uptight and a bit overbearing with her kids, but she's honestly not as bad as Dede and shouldn't be compared to her.

More Info on Claire

(3) Gloria Delgado-Pritchett

Gloria is from Colombia. She has a sister, Sonia. Gloria is funny and kind. BUT she stole Sonia's life from her, including Jay (who noticed Sonia first).

More Info on Gloria

(4) Haley Dunphy

As a teen, she loved being the popular girl in high school and partying (but was never particularly good in school). However, as she grew up, she made efforts to mature and now has twins with Dylan.
