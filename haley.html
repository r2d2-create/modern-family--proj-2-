(4) Haley Dunphy

As a teen, she loved being the popular girl in high school and partying (but was never particularly good in school). However, as she grew up, she made efforts to mature and now has twins with Dylan. I'm really happy that she's finally coming into her own and has both a stable job with good benefits (though at a snake oil company) and is learning to balance that with being an attentive mom.
